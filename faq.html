FAQs

You've got questions, we've got answers!

What do you sell?

We are a bagels shop selling bagels by the bag, cream cheese, hot and cold bagel sandwiches, drinks and assorted extras

What are these "extras"?

Bialys, babka, tubs of house made flavored cream cheeses, including vegan spreads, Acme whitefish salad, egg salad, pickles. We are adding more weekly.

Do you offer online ordering? What about delivery?

Yes. We accept online orders for Weds, Thurs, Fri between 7 AM and 1:30 PM for pick up anytime that day. Ordering is currently limited to. bags of bagels and tubs of cream cheese for pickup. We may offer delivery in the future if we find a partner that offers reasonable delivery fees to our customers.

Can I place large catering orders?

This is in progress. We will be installing a catering ordering system that will allow larger orders planned in advance. Please use our Contact form below to ask us for updates.

Are Marigold Bagels vegan?

Yes, our bagel dough is vegan! We use only plant-based ingredients in our dough, with no dairy, eggs, or honey. The exceptions are our Jalapeño Cheddar and Asiago Cheese bagels, which contain cheese. Also, we make our own cashew based spread flavored with only plant based ingredients that is unique and full of flavor.

Why do you sometimes sell out before 2pm?

That's a good question. We used to but its very rare now since we increased our daily production. But it does happen occasionally when there is an unusual demand on a given day. Best bet is to plan on arriving before 10 AM on those days. We rarely sell out at that time.

Why do you run out? Don't you bake all day?

Yes, we bake throughout the morning on busy days. But without an unlimited supply of bagels to bake, we occasionally will sell out. We've had some requests to bake more slowly so as to stretch out a limited supply. But that simply makes customers who arrive early have to wait longer.

Where is your Centrilized Dough Distribution Center?

Unfortunately, we are not a sophisticated enough operation to have such a supply line in place. All of our bagels go from bags of flour to finished bagels over 4 days in our shop in a space of about 800 square feet.
