Our story

Classic bagel craft with a sunny neighborhood spirit.

Marigold Bagels brings the chew, shine, and comfort of a New York-style bagel shop into a relaxed San Diego bakery.

Built around the first bite.

Our favorite bagels have a glossy crust, a tender inside, and enough character to carry simple toppings. That is the idea behind Marigold: honest bakery work, cheerful service, and food that makes a regular morning feel a little more cared for.

We make bagels for people on their way to class, families picking up brunch, coworkers sharing a dozen, and anyone who believes breakfast should have personality.

What matters

Simple ingredients, clear flavors, kind service.

Fresh Every Day

Bagels are boiled, baked, and restocked through the morning so the counter feels alive.

Community First

We want the shop to feel easy to step into, whether you are ordering one coffee or trays for fifty.

Playful Specials

From rainbow bagels to seasonal cream cheese, there is always room for a little color.

Freshness note

The best selection is before noon. Popular flavors can sell out on busy weekends.
